## About the business

Rank One Trading describes itself as a Chennai-based trading and distribution company serving individual customers, dealers, corporate buyers and manufacturers. Its public offering brings several practical product categories into one business:

- **EV charging equipment:** portable and wall-mounted chargers, selected commercial charging solutions, cables and accessories. The business describes itself as an authorised Strompuls EV-charger distributor for Tamil Nadu and Kerala.
- **D’Cal water conditioning:** hard-water products intended to address mineral scale in tanks, fixtures and appliances. The company describes itself as an authorised D’Cal distributor.
- **Home essentials:** a growing assortment of cleaning, laundry, personal and home-use products listed in the online shop.
- **Commercial and dealer enquiries:** advice, bulk orders and distribution relationships alongside regular retail shopping.

The official site identifies its Chennai contact address as **41, Azizuddin Khan Bahadur Street, Chennai – 600005**, and provides **info@rankonetrading.in** and **+91 94449 12459** as contact details. Availability, prices, warranties and technical capabilities must be checked against the current product listing or manufacturer documentation.

## The problem

The business sells products that answer **very different customer questions**. An EV owner wants to know charger type, power rating, vehicle compatibility, electrical requirements and installation support. A household dealing with hard-water deposits wants an explanation of a conditioner, suitable tank capacity, use instructions and expected maintenance. A commercial buyer may need quantities, warranty information or a tailored quote.

A conventional general-products catalogue can bury these distinctions. The website needs a straightforward division between product families, useful education around unfamiliar technology, visible purchase actions and an alternative contact route for buyers who require guidance before ordering.

This describes the needs evident in the current product offering. The supplied project media documents both an earlier and a redesigned website presentation, but does not by itself establish measured business improvement.

## How Tech Conversion presented the website

The following points describe **visible elements of the published site and supplied project media**. They should not be read as an unverified internal development timeline or proof that every draft variation was deployed.

1. **Establish recognisable product routes.** The website distinguishes **EV Chargers**, **Hard Water Solutions** and broader home-product categories so different buyers can start with the most relevant section.
2. **Lead with a specific problem and solution.** The D’Cal presentation introduces hard-water concerns and the conditioning product with a direct action to explore that solution.
3. **Give EV charging its own section.** The [EV Chargers](https://rankonetrading.in/ev-chargers/) page separates portable, wall-mounted, commercial and accessory options, with product cards and information about choosing charging capacity.
4. **Explain a less familiar product category.** The [D’Cal](https://rankonetrading.in/dcal/) page presents household hard-water concerns, the company's description of its conditioning product and the available D’Cal range.
5. **Support regular shopping and discovery.** The [Shop](https://rankonetrading.in/shop/) includes product categories, prices, brand filters, availability controls and add-to-cart actions for available products.
6. **Balance consumer purchasing with expert help.** The website highlights consultation and bulk-order enquiry routes for customers who need advice about a vehicle, electrical supply, water system or larger commercial order.
7. **Maintain a consistent product-led visual identity.** The redesign uses structured product sections, strong category cues, product photography and clear calls to action.
8. **Treat technical claims responsibly.** Product performance, compatibility, distribution status and warranty claims should be checked against the current seller and manufacturer documentation.

## Standards the website should be reviewed against

These are **quality and compliance review criteria**, not claims that an audit was performed or a certificate independently checked.

- **[Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals):** test product-grid loading, large hero assets, mobile performance and interaction stability.
- **[WCAG 2.2 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/):** review keyboard navigation, category controls, focus states, contrast, cart controls and form labels.
- **[Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials):** review discoverable product categories, product details, helpful internal linking and search metadata.
- **[Schema.org](https://schema.org/):** evaluate accurate Product, Offer, BreadcrumbList and Organisation markup where the live implementation supports it.
- **Electrical product accuracy:** verify charger power ratings, connector and vehicle compatibility, outdoor ratings, installation requirements, applicable Indian electrical standards and manufacturer warranty documentation.
- **Water-conditioning claim accuracy:** check product performance, material safety, test reports and suitability against manufacturer evidence. A claim to reduce scale does not automatically establish that dissolved mineral hardness is removed.
- **Ecommerce trust and privacy:** review applicable consumer-protection requirements, shipping/returns consistency, personal-data handling and payment-service configuration.
